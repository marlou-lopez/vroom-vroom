# Command: average

Session consistency. The corners in the facts are where the corner time varies most between laps; each
has the slowest and the fastest lap side by side, so coach what differs between them (an own-lap cue fits
well: "brake where you braked on lap 5"). Every finding has kind `inconsistent` and `time_s` is the spread
of that corner's time. `focus` is null and `encouragement` is null.
